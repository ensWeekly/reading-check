Sound & Word Discovery

A calm, teacher-guided learning check

Welcome!

We will play some short sound and word games. You can listen, point, or say your answer. It is okay to say “I don't know.”

A teacher should sit with the learner. This is an informal screening activity, not a standardised assessment or diagnosis. No information is uploaded or saved online.

Teacher: select an answer when applicable, then record whether the student answered independently, with help, or not yet. For oral responses, record the outcome directly. Never tell the learner the answer before marking an independent attempt.

Session summary

These are descriptive observations only. Do not interpret the total as a reading age, IQ, disability diagnosis, or validated score.
